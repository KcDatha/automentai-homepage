06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity before we talk.

01How long does a build take?+

Most scoped builds take 2–4 weeks once we have access, content and approvals. Complex integrations can take longer; your plan sets the schedule.

02What do I need to provide?+

Your service details, pricing, policies, brand assets, calendar and tool access, plus one person who can approve decisions. We guide you through a checklist.

03Do you replace the tools I already use?+

Usually not. We connect your existing CRM, calendars, booking and payment tools where practical. If a replacement makes sense, we agree on the migration plan with you first.

04Who owns my website and business data?+

You do. Your accounts, website and customer data stay under your control. Third-party subscriptions remain subject to their own terms.

05What happens if the agent does not know an answer?+

It hands off to a person. Agents use approved documents and live systems, log conversations and escalate uncertain answers, discounts, refunds and event decisions.
